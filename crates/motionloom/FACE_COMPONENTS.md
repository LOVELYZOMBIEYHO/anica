# Face components

FaceLayout is an attribute-free container for Eye, Eyebrow, Nose, Mouth, and Ear.
The previous flat attributes are removed, not deprecated. There are no implicit
eyes, nose, mouth, or ears. Each component has a nonempty ID, unique within its
FaceLayout. The ID spelling does not determine its position or anatomy.

```xml
<FaceLayout>
  <Eye id="eye_a" position={[-0.316,-0.149,0]}
       width="0.350" opening="0.210" tilt="-4"
       socketWidth="0.550" socketHeight="0.510" socketDepth="0.015">
    <Texture asset="sclera_image" />
    <Iris id="iris_a" position={[0,0,0]} shape="ellipse" scale={[0.78,1]}
          radius="0.082" pupilRadius="0.032">
      <Texture asset="iris_image" />
    </Iris>
    <Eyeliner id="upper_a" edge="upper" thickness="0.014"
             span={[0,1]} taper={[0.85,0.20]}
             extension={[0,0.028]} tipLift={[0,0.012]} />
    <Eyeliner id="lower_a" edge="lower" thickness="0.003"
             span={[0.12,0.88]} taper={[0.50,0.10]}
             extension={[0,0]} tipLift={[0,0]} />
  </Eye>
  <Eye id="eye_b" position={[0.316,-0.149,0]}
       width="0.350" opening="0.210"
       socketWidth="0.550" socketHeight="0.510" socketDepth="0.015" />
  <Nose id="nose" position={[0,-0.363,0]}
        length="0.180" width="0.100" projection="0.060" />
  <Mouth id="mouth" position={[0,-0.561,0]}
         width="0.086" opening="0.014" />
</FaceLayout>
```

Declare image references as ImageAsset nodes. Native paths and Base64 data URIs use the
same image resolver as MaterialAsset textures.

## Coordinates and generator limits

For facialCage, X/Y locate a feature on the front HeadProfile surface. Eye Z
moves the generated eyeball and its Iris relative to that surface; it does not
deform the surrounding head skin. Eyeliner remains attached to the eyelid edge.
Use socketDepth for the recessed orbital skin. Positive socketDepth increases
the recess. Other component Z values
remain surface offsets. This is surface placement, not absolute world XYZ.
Eye tilt uses degrees. Arbitrary
counts of front-facing eyes and mouths are supported, subject to non-overlapping
sampled socket patches. Overlapping and undersampled patches are rejected.

Ear relief attaches to the lateral surface: X chooses the side, Y sets height,
and Z sets its center along head depth. Width spans head depth, height spans Y,
and depth controls outward relief. This is a simple ear field, not a detailed
helix mesh. S86 keeps its existing custom ear geometry.

Procedural topology retains normalized head-field coordinates. This release
does not introduce arbitrary oriented openings on the back of the head,
automatic blink animation, or new custom-cage child tags.

## Texture ownership

Each surface component accepts one optional Texture referencing an ImageAsset.
Its generated image surface belongs to the head mesh and follows the component
dimensions and placement after DSL edits. A direct Eye Texture covers the convex
eyeball/sclera. An Iris Texture covers only the optional iris disc. An Eyeliner
Texture covers that one lid-bound ribbon. Iris is optional; omitting it leaves a
complete eyeball without an iris. Eye accepts at most one Iris and any number of
independently identified Eyeliner children.

Texture accepts offset={[0,0]}, scale={[1,1]}, rotation="0". These attributes
transform image sampling only; they never reshape or move the component geometry.
Offset is applied after image scale, so it remains effective at every scale.
Positive scale expands image coverage, negative scale mirrors the image, and
rotation uses degrees. Zero scale is rejected. Untextured Eye surfaces inherit
the head material.

Iris position is an Eye-local XYZ offset. Iris shape accepts circle, ellipse, or
square. Radius is its base half-size and Iris scale={[x,y]} changes geometry:
circle/ellipse use a curved boundary, while square uses four straight boundaries.
Ellipse defaults to scale={[1,1.25]}; the other shapes default to {[1,1]}.
The generated pupil follows Iris position and geometry scale. Eyeliner edge is
upper or lower; span and taper are normalized pairs, while extension and tipLift
are eye-local distances. Multiple ribbons on one edge are valid.

The removed Eye attributes irisRadius, irisOcclusion, upperLidFold, cornerTaper,
and eyelidRim are rejected. Move irisRadius to Iris.radius and define the pupil
with Iris.pupilRadius. Lid-line appearance belongs to Eyeliner children.

## Parametric eyebrows

Ordinary eyebrows use ribbons generated by Rust, without Vertex or
Face data. Custom geometry can still use MeshAsset.

```xml
<Eyebrow id="brow_a" position={[-0.316,0.080,0]}
         width="0.300" thickness="0.018" arch="0.035" tilt="5">
  <Texture asset="s86_brow_left" offset={[0,0]} scale={[1,1.2]} rotation="0" />
</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow id="brow_b" position={[0.316,0.080,0]}
         width="0.300" thickness="0.018" arch="0.035" tilt="-5" />
```

Place these inside FaceLayout. The second example deliberately has no Texture
and inherits the head material. X/Y locate the ribbon on the head front surface;
Z adds separation. The generator adds a small surface offset to avoid z-fighting.
Width is the endpoint span, thickness is ribbon height, arch is midpoint rise
above the endpoints, and tilt rotates the ribbon in degrees. Negative arch is
allowed. Defaults are position [0,0,0], width 0.300, thickness 0.018, arch 0.035,
and tilt 0. Width and thickness must be positive.

UVs run from left to right and top to bottom across the curved ribbon. Editing
width or thickness carries the image with the geometry. Eyebrow Texture transforms
operate within this ribbon: scale enlarges the image, offset translates it in UV
spans, and rotation rotates it in degrees; these do not resize the ribbon itself.
Transparent padding in an image still affects the visible eyebrow thickness.
S86 keeps its embedded ImageAssets and replaces both explicit eyebrow cards and
their Model references with these components. Its head profile is unchanged.

## Flat layout migration

Move eyeHeight to Eye position Y and eyeSpacing/2 to positive/negative X.
Move eyeWidth, eyeOpening, and eyeSocket fields to each Eye without the eye
prefix. Move noseHeight and mouthHeight to their component position Y. Copy
all dimensions before tuning, then remove every flat FaceLayout attribute.

Head fitting proposals patch Eye/Nose/Mouth by component ID. Optimization group
names include Eye.width, Eye.positionY, Eye.pairSpacing, Nose.positionY, and
Mouth.opening. These are fitting controls, not additional DSL attributes.
The group fitter currently adjusts all members of a family together; independent
multi-eye fitting needs a future per-component objective. Geometry inspection
and silhouette comparison can inspect such heads now.

S86 keeps Base64 textures in main.motionloom. Its two independent eye card
assets are replaced by nested Texture bindings. HeadProfile, HeadDome, and
the facial cage resolution remain authored in the head.
